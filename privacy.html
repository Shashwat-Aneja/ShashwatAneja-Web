SITE / PRIVACY

Privacy.

Last updated: 10 November 2026. This notice describes how the personal portfolio at shashwataneja.com handles visitor information. The website is operated by Shashwat Aneja. For privacy enquiries, email connect@shashwataneja.com.

What the website collects

The portfolio does not offer visitor accounts, payments, or a server-side contact form. It does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. The website's delivery and security infrastructure may process technical information such as your IP address, requested pages, browser details, request time and security events. Infrastructure providers and their precise log-retention settings are under review; these technical records may be processed independently of the browser preferences described below.

If you contact me

When you email me, I receive the address you use, the message you send and any information or attachments you voluntarily include. I use this information to read and respond to your enquiry and handle relevant follow-up. Email delivery and storage involve the services used by you and me; their processing and retention policies also apply. Contacting me does not subscribe you to marketing.

Browser preferences

The site stores two small preferences in your browser's local storage: shashwat-theme (your selected appearance) and shashwat-privacy-notice-seen (whether you dismissed the privacy notice). These preferences are not used to build a server-side visitor profile. They remain until you clear this site's browser data or change the relevant setting.

Image workspace

If you choose a file in the portfolio's Image Lab, the current implementation processes that image in your browser using a canvas and does not send its contents to a site upload endpoint. Avoid using sensitive images on shared or untrusted devices.

Other services

Following links to services such as GitHub and LinkedIn takes you to third-party websites with their own privacy practices. The domain uses Cloudflare's DNS/network infrastructure. The website's origin hosting and email-processing arrangements are still being verified, so this page will be updated with confirmed provider and retention details rather than assumptions.

Retention and deletion

Browser preferences remain on your device until cleared. Correspondence is kept while reasonably needed to respond, maintain the relevant conversation and meet applicable legal obligations; it is reviewed for deletion when no longer needed. Hosting and security logs may follow provider-controlled retention settings. Some records may need to be retained where law requires it, even if you ask for erasure.

Your privacy choices and requests

To request access to, correction of, or erasure of personal information that I control, to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or to raise a privacy concern, email connect@shashwataneja.com with the subject Privacy request. Include the email address or correspondence involved and a brief explanation. I may verify your connection to the data before disclosing or changing it, without requesting unnecessary identity documents. Requests are assessed under applicable law and any required retention obligations.

Grievances

Send privacy grievances to the same address with the subject Privacy grievance. I aim to respond as soon as practicable and no later than 90 days where the applicable DPDP grievance-response requirement applies. This contact is for the portfolio at shashwataneja.com; separate services may publish their own privacy notices.

Updates

This page will be updated when verified processing arrangements or website functionality change. Its last-updated date will show when the text changes.

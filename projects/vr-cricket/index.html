VR Cricket

A physical-input experiment connecting a real bat movement to a virtual cricket experience.

TYPEUNITY / VR / SENSORS
FOCUSMOTION + PHYSICS
THE QUESTIONCan a physical movement become a game mechanic?
MOTION + PHYSICS
VRSENSOR → BAT → WORLD
WHAT I BUILT

A Unity-based VR cricket experiment using motion sensing hardware to track bat movement and feed that movement into the virtual experience.

HOW IT WORKS

The physical controller provides movement data. That data is interpreted as bat motion inside Unity, where the virtual bat and ball respond through gameplay and physics. The interesting boundary is the translation between a physical gesture and a digital action.

REFLECTION

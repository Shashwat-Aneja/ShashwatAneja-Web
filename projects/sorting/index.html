WHAT I BUILT

A visualizer for Bubble Sort, Insertion Sort and Selection Sort that exposes their behaviour through animated bars and measurable operations.

HOW IT WORKS

The same collection of values becomes the visual field. The algorithm controls which bars are compared or moved, turning abstract complexity into visible rhythm. Controls let the visitor change the amount and arrangement of data before watching the algorithm work.

ARRAYCOMPARESWAPSORT
WHAT I LEARNED
REFLECTION

Two algorithms can reach the same sorted result while behaving completely differently on the way there. Visualization makes that difference immediate.

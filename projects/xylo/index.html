WHAT I BUILT

An AI-assisted business automation backend exploring invoice OCR, accounting, reporting and scheduling as connected workflows.

HOW IT WORKS

The system treats business information as a pipeline. Unstructured input is interpreted, transformed into structured records and then made useful through accounting, reporting or workflow actions. The value is in connecting the stages, not in any one screen.
